Relief
Raised sits proud of the plate. Inlaid is routed into it but stays its own metal, with just a thin seam at the edge. Engraved cuts in and flattens to the plate's own tone. Unmatch to set the lettering, frame rails, corner bolts and badge pieces each on their own — a raised badge over inlaid lettering, say.
Plate
Custom Colour
The custom colour sits outside the finish groups because it applies to any of them: metal and powder coat take it directly, and the wood and stone photos are remapped onto it so their grain survives the recolour. Clear it to put the finish back on its own colours.
Trim & Lettering
Unmatch to colour the lettering, frame rails, corner bolts and the badge's delta and bars separately — the original artwork carries each in its own colourway. A part set to engraved relief ignores its colour — an engraved cut is filled with the plate's own tone.
Roster Columns
One name per line. Start a line with ## to run a second heading partway down a column.
Dedication Quote
The quote block hangs from its last baseline and grows upward; the attribution rides that baseline and moves relative to it.
Era
